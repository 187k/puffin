hello@puffin.app

Puffin is a pet-care app that brings health tracking, vaccination and feeding reminders, daily activity tracking and a nearby vet-clinic finder into one place — so you can look after your pet without juggling notes, chats and calendars.

Puffin is designed with cats and dogs in mind, and you can add other furry, feathered or scaly companions too. Each pet gets their own profile, health timeline and reminders.

Choose a reminder type — vaccine, medication, feeding, grooming or walks — then set a time and how often it repeats. Puffin sends a gentle nudge at the right moment and keeps a record once it's done.

With your permission, Puffin uses your location to show veterinary clinics and animal healthcare services nearby, including distance, opening hours and directions.

Your pet's records belong to you. We only use your data to power the features you use, and you can export or delete it whenever you like.
